I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Engineering at Northwestern University and a visiting researcher at Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory (Fermilab). My research focuses on fault-tolerant hardware design, reconfigurable architectures, on-device learning, and hardware-algorithm co-design.

I build resource-efficient, fault-resilient neural network accelerators and the compilation frameworks that target them. My work spans automated ML compilation toolchains, fixed-point on-device backpropagation, post-silicon ASIC bring-up, and hardware reliability for edge computing platforms.

## Research Interests

* Fault-tolerant ML accelerators for edge devices
* On-device learning under fixed-point constraints
* Reconfigurable architectures, FPGA, and high-level synthesis
* ML compilers and hardware-algorithm co-design
